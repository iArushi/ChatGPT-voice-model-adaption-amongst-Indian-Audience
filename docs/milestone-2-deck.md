## Slide 1 — Chosen segment and why it matters

**Title:** Working professionals in India (ChatGPT mobile)

**Segments we considered**

| Segment | Why they might use voice | Why we did not lead with them |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Students & young learners | Long prompts, homework help | Lower willingness to pay; mixed English/Hinglish hurts trust on first try |
| Job seekers | Cover letters, interview prep | Spiky usage; voice is nice-to-have vs typing polish |
| Content creators | Scripts, captions, ideas | Smaller share of mobile DAU in India vs general productivity |
| Regional-language-first users | Speech beats typing in Hindi/regional | Needs recognition investment; not a 2-week fix |
| **Working professionals (chosen)** | Email drafts, summaries, analysis on the go | **Daily use, work stakes, Plus/subscription fit, already on mobile** |

**Why highest impact**

- Among the heaviest ChatGPT mobile users in our sample.
- Prompts are **high stakes** (client mail, reports)—a wrong word changes the task.
- If voice becomes safe, **session length and repeat use** move; that is where subscription value shows up.
- Fixing **control** here teaches patterns that later help students and creators too.

## Slide 2 — Research insights (quant + qual)

**Methods**

- Short survey (Google Form), shared in professional networks: **n = 16** total, **n = 10** working professionals in India who use ChatGPT on phone.
- **3** follow-up interviews completed + survey open-text; **3 more** planned (same guide in repo).

**Quantitative (survey, focus segment n = 10)**

| Finding | Share |
|---------|-------|
| Already know where the mic is / have tried voice at least once | ~9/10 |
| **Typing feels safer or more in control than voice** | **70%** |
| **Would use voice more if they could check and fix text before it sends** | **50%** |
| Use voice regularly on other apps (e.g. WhatsApp voice notes) | **80%** |
| Rate ChatGPT voice **better** than Google / Gemini | **0%** (same or worse) |

**Qualitative — pain points & blockers**

1. **Commit anxiety:** “Voice sends it—I can’t fix ‘role’ vs ‘roll’ before ChatGPT answers.”
2. **Typing is the edit surface:** With the keyboard, correction is continuous; with voice, correction is after the fact (or never).
3. **WhatsApp mental model:** Listen → re-record → then send. ChatGPT feels like **send-first**.
4. **Gemini comparison:** Live conversation allows interruption; ChatGPT mobile composer feels **one-shot**.
5. **Privacy / open office:** Less about the mic icon; more about **others hearing** and **wrong text going out**.
6. **Not an accent project (for this cohort):** English-first professionals blamed **flow**, not Hindi recognition—still want a **review step**.

**Sample note:** Target was 30+ survey responses; treat quant as **directional** until scaled.

## Slide 3 — Problem Framing Canvas

| Canvas field | Our answer |
|--------------|------------|
| **User / context** | Working professional in India, ChatGPT **mobile**, multitasking (commute, between meetings), writing work-related prompts |
| **Current behaviour** | Knows voice exists; tries occasionally; **defaults to typing** for anything important |
| **Desired behaviour** | Use voice for long or hands-busy prompts **when it feels as safe as typing** |
| **Barrier (functional)** | Transcript is committed (or feels committed) **before** read-and-fix |
| **Barrier (emotional)** | Fear of looking wrong to the model / colleague overhead; loss of control |
| **Barrier (social)** | Speaking prompts in shared spaces; rather type quietly |
| **Competitive reference** | WhatsApp: review before send. Gemini Live: mid-utterance correction |
| **Problem statement** | Voice on ChatGPT mobile is underused **not because it is hidden**, but because **users cannot trust the first transcript** for high-stakes work prompts |
| **How we might solve (direction)** | **Voice Draft Mode**—speech lands in an editable draft; Send is explicit; uncertain words visible |
| **Out of scope (for now)** | New ASR model, full Hinglish, auto-send “cleanup,” Advanced Voice Mode parity |
| **Success signal (leading)** | % voice sessions that reach **Send** after edit (draft-to-send), not just mic tap |
