# Milestone 1 — Market research (summary)

Full deck: [ChatGPT Voice Adaption amongst Indian Audience.pdf](../ChatGPT%20Voice%20Adaption%20amongst%20Indian%20Audience.pdf)

## Landscape (India)

- Mobile-first; voice familiar from **WhatsApp**, **Google Assistant**, **voice search**  
- Multilingual context: English for work, Hindi/regional for social—recognition matters but **adoption already exists** in other apps  
- ChatGPT mobile competes with **Gemini Live** on “feels conversational”

## Segments (initial)

1. Non-English / vernacular-first  
2. Tier-2 & Tier-3 mobile-first  
3. WhatsApp voice-note natives  
4. Students & young learners  
5. Metro English-first **professionals** → carried into Milestone 2 deep dive  

## Competitive UX patterns

| Product | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| WhatsApp | Record → **listen** → send or re-record |
| Gemini Live | **Interrupt** and correct mid-stream |
| Google Assistant | Short commands; less “long prompt” |
| ChatGPT mobile | Mic → transcript → **feels immediate** |

## Metrics explored (Milestone 1)

- Weekly Successful Voice Users (WSVU)  
- Discovery rate · Voice start rate · Task success · Repeat usage
