# Survey & interviews — Milestone 2

## Survey (quantitative)

**Audience filter:** India · uses ChatGPT on **mobile** · employed (full-time or freelance) · 18+

**Distribution:** LinkedIn, WhatsApp professional groups, alumni networks (Bengaluru + remote India)

**Sample:** 16 total responses · **10** match focus segment (working professionals)

### Questions (short form)

1. How often do you use ChatGPT on your phone? (Daily / Weekly / Rarely)  
2. Have you used **voice input** in ChatGPT mobile? (Yes often / Tried once or twice / Know it exists, never / Didn’t know)  
3. What do you mainly use ChatGPT for on phone? (open)  
4. **Typing vs voice:** Which feels **safer and more in control** for work-related prompts?  
5. What stops you from using voice more? (multi-select: accuracy, privacy, can’t fix text, habit, don’t know where mic is, other)  
6. Would **checking and editing the transcript before send** make you use voice more? (Yes / Maybe / No)  
7. Do you use voice on **WhatsApp** or **Google Assistant**? How does ChatGPT compare? (open)  
8. City + role (optional)

## Hypotheses (focus segment)

Before fieldwork we assumed voice was low because:

1. **H1 — Discoverability:** Users cannot find the mic → *weak* (most had tried voice).  
2. **H2 — Control:** Transcript commits before review → *strong* (70% typing safer; 50% want check-and-fix).  
3. **H3 — Accuracy / accent:** Indian English/Hinglish ASR poor → *mixed* (secondary in this English-work cohort).  
4. **H4 — Privacy:** Open offices / family around → *moderate* (typing wins in public).  
5. **H5 — Habit:** Keyboard is default for “serious” prompts → *strong* (aligned with H2).

**Winner for this segment:** H2 + H5 (control + habit), not H1.

## Qualitative interviews

**Target:** 5–6 users in focus segment · **Completed at time of write-up:** 3 follow-up calls + open-text survey answers · **Guide below** for remaining sessions.

**Recruitment:** Same filter as survey; 25–35 min video or phone; incentive: none (peer network).

**Guide (topics)**

- Last time they used ChatGPT voice on mobile—what happened?  
- Walk through typing a work prompt vs speaking it—where does voice break?  
- Show WhatsApp voice note flow—what would ChatGPT need to copy?  
- Gemini / Google experience if any  
- What would “success” look like for voice on ChatGPT?

### Themes (from 3 calls + survey open-text)

| Source | Role (approx.) | Quote / theme |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| Call 1 | Marketing manager | “It heard ‘band’ not ‘brand’. I retyped the whole thing.” |
| Call 2 | Consultant | “On WhatsApp I listen before it goes. ChatGPT feels like it already sent.” |
| Call 3 | Product analyst | “Gemini I can interrupt. Here I stop and type if it’s client-facing.” |
| Survey (open-text) | Multiple | Keyboard = fix as you go; fear of wrong prompt to model |
| Survey (open-text) | Multiple | Mic known; not a discoverability issue |

**Synthesis:** Blocker cluster is **pre-send control**, not discoverability. Secondary: environment (office). Recognition mattered more in open-text from bilingual respondents than in the three calls.
