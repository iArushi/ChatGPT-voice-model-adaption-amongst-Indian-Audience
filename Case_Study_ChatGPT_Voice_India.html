Voice Draft Mode
Indians already trust voice on WhatsApp and Google. On ChatGPT mobile, the gap is not “find the mic” — it is control: the transcript can commit before they read and fix it.

Focus segment

Working professionals in India on ChatGPT mobile — daily use, high-stakes prompts, subscription upside.

Solution

Voice Draft Mode — speech stays in an editable draft until the user taps Send.

Market research

Milestone 1 — India voice landscape

Mobile-first India already uses voice for search, assistants, and WhatsApp notes. ChatGPT competes with Gemini Live on conversational feel — but long prompts need a different UX than short commands.

ProductVoice pattern
WhatsAppRecord → listen → send or re-record
Gemini LiveInterrupt and correct mid-stream
Google AssistantShort commands
ChatGPT mobileMic → transcript → feels immediate

Segments explored: vernacular-first · tier-2/3 mobile · WhatsApp natives · students · metro professionals → Milestone 2 deep dive
